[…] Send the agenda with enough lead time for people to prepare, then adjust that timing to the complexity of the meeting. A routine status check can go out close to the meeting if the context is already familiar. A budget review, vendor decision, or cross-functional planning session needs more time with the materials so people can read, compare, and come ready to decide.
